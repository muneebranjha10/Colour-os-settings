# ColourOS Battery v1.1 RC8 — Classic Flow
Made by Muneeb.

- Classic green glass, original RC3 emerald body palette and neutral silver selectable.
- Gentler glass motion, Smooth +22% base timing and Relaxed +60%; slower same-window health transition.
- Shared adaptive settings rows, aligned chevrons and status text, no empty native groups.
- Health rereads every 30s (60s low power) while open and focused, with source and timestamp. No background service. Live Samsung/kernel counters prioritized; bracketed saved health and usage parsed, saved ASOC separate from live. Manual snapshot explicitly pauses polling until refresh.
- Cycle counters are firmware-owned; they need not change on every reread or charger connection. Unreadable sensors stay unavailable. Multi-battery arrays are not guessed.
- 30 JVM regression checks and source wiring checks, signing and LSPosed entry verification. Hardware compatibility, visual smoothness, and sensor accuracy still need device testing. Prior Balanced mode concern unchanged. Samsung Device Care scope only.
